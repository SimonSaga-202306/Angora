## Tailored Solutions, Global Technology

Angora Networks has rapidly completed product development and production stages with its own equity since 2016, the year it was founded and started its promotional activities with its identity as a domestic network device manufacturer and added many well-established institutions among its references in a short time. Angora Networks has achieved this success thanks to its expert staff with decades of knowledge in the industry.

* * *

Angora Networks has developed the necessary infrastructure to create products that comply with industry standards, operate from day one, are physically and technologically sound and ready for sale. Thus, Angora Networks product portfolio consists of high-tech and innovative products at global standards that meet the real needs of its customers. Angora Networks products enable their customers to direct their limited resources to the right solutions.

Angora Networks shares its know-how in the field of communication networks (Network) obtained from R&D activities with the whole business ecosystem as Professional Services.
Online support and constantly updated documentation of the products allow users to commission and operate their devices very quickly and safely.
With its dealer and service network spreading through 81 cities, it is committed to the shortest service levels for all its products.
