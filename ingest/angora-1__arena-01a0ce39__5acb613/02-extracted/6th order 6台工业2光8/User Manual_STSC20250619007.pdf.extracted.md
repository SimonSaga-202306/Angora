Usage Guide 
Please prepare 
 
● Category 5e or better cable for RJ45 ports 
● Fiber cables(patchcords)and modules for fiber ports 
● Installation tools(screwdrivers, ESD wrist strap, pliers 
etc.) 
● Wear an ESD wrist strap 
Make sure the wrist strap makes good skin contact and is 
reliably grounded 
 
● Mount the switch  
Make sure the spring of the DIN rail mounting bracket 
compresses against the upper edge of the DIN rail 
 
● Secure the position 
Rotate the switch down toward the DIN rail until the DIN 
rail mounting bracket clicks 
Step 1：Installing the din switch 
Step 2：Connecting the grounding cable 
The power input end of the switch has a noise filter, whose 
central ground is directly connected to the chassis to form 
the chassis ground. 
 
You must securely connect this chassis ground to the earth 
so the faradism and leakage electricity can be released to 
the earth to minimize EMI susceptibility of the switch.  
 
Correctly connecting the grounding cable for the switch is vital 
to lightning protection and EMI protection. 
Step 3：Connecting power cords 
● Make sure each power cord has a separate circuit 
breaker. 
● Before connecting a power cord, make sure the circui t 
breaker for the power cord is turned off. 
● Din-rail industrial Ethernet switches support redundant 
dual DC power supply. You can connect one or two DC 
power supply according to your requirement. 
To avoid connection mistakes, identify the positive(+)
and negative(-)marks above the DC power receptacle 
for the terminal block connection. 
Installation procedure as follows  
1. Make sure the switch power off 
2. Correctly connecting the grounding cable for the switch 
3. Connect the DC power to the positive(+)and negative(-)
of the terminal block(red cord connect “+”, black cord 
connect “-”), fasten the screws through the screw-driver 
4. Turn on and check the power indicator on the front pan-
el of the device. If the power indicator is on, the power 
supply is working normally. 
Step 4：Verifying the installation 
After you complete the installation, verify the following 
information: 
1. There is enough space around the switch for the heat 
dissipation. 
2. The DIN rail/mounting ears are securely installed. 
3. The grounding cable is connected correctly. 
4. If the power supply is properly applied as required 
by the switch. 
5. The power cords are correctly connected. 
6. If an interface cable for a port is routed outdoors, 
verify that a network port lightning protector is used 
for the port.  
7. If a power line is routed from outdoors, verify that a 
surge protected power strip is used for the switch.
