Business Plan for  
Connexite: Selling Networking Switches and SFP Units  
on Amazon UK 
Executive Summary 
Connexite (connexite.co.uk) is a UK-based tech startup specializing in high-quality, managed 
industrial and commercial networking switches and SFP units. Our product line includes the 8-
port (CNX-2208-PG), 24-port (CNX-2224-PG), and 48-port (CNX-2248-PG) L2+ PoE+ 
managed switches, along with 1G LX (CNX-1G-LX) and 10G SR (CNX-10G-SR) SFP units. 
Leveraging Amazon’s Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) program, we aim to target small to 
medium-sized businesses (SMBs), IT professionals, and enterprises in the UK and Europe. This 
plan outlines the FBA process, a robust digital marketing strategy, and a competitive pricing 
strategy with launching offers to establish market presence and drive initial sales. 
 
1. Amazon FBA Process for Connexite 
Overview of Amazon FBA 
Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) allows sellers to store inventory in Amazon’s fulfillment centers, 
where Amazon handles picking, packing, shipping, customer service, and returns. This enables 
Connexite to offer Prime shipping, enhancing visibility and customer trust.sell.amazon.co.uk
