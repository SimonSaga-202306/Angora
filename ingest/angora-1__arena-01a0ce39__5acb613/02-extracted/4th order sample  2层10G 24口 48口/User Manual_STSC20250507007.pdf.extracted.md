Usage Guide 
Type 1：Desktop Use ● Connecting the DC power cord 
● Connecting the AC power cord 
 
1. Connect one end of the power cord to the AC 
power plug of the device accurately and in place. 
2. Connect the other end of the power cord to an 
external AC power outlet. 
The standalone and rack switches are sharing the 
same configuration cable connection method. 
Please follow below steps to complete the connection. 
1. Connect the USB plug of the configuration cable 
to the USB port of the computer. 
2. Connect the RJ45 end of the configuration cable 
to the Console port of the Ethernet switch. 
1. Make sure that the device is disconnected from 
the incoming power. 
2. Connect one end of the protective grounding 
cable to the grounding screw of the equipment, 
and the other end is well grounded  
3. Insert the DC end of the DC terminal of the 
power adapter into the DC socket of the device, 
and connect the other end to the mains supply. 
4. Turn on the power supply and check the power 
light on the front panel of the device.  
5. If the indicator light is on, it indicates that the 
power supply is normal. 
All in all, there are usually 3 types of installation 
methods for Ethernet switches  
Desktop, Wall-mounted and Rack-mounted 
Either way, there are some precautions  
 
● Please make sure the switch is working under 
a correct and stable voltage. 
● Before using the switch, be sure to ground it 
via the ground screw. 
● Before cleaning the switch, the power plug of 
the switch should be pulled out first.  
● Please do not wipe the switch with a wet 
cloth, and do not use liquid to clean the 
switch.  
Directly place the device on the platform or desktop, 
then do the correct power and cable connection. 
Type 2：Wall-mounted Use 
Place the switch at the location to be installed, and 
use a screwdriver to tighten the mounting ear 
screws on both sides. 
1. Please make sure the 19’’rack on good grounding. 
2. Fixing the mounting ears on the both side of the 
switch. 
3. Place the switch on a bracket of the rack and 
move the switch along the guide rails of the rack 
to a proper position. 
4. Use screws to install the Ethernet switch on the 
rack. 
Type 3：Rack-mounted Use 
When installing the switch on the rack, there must 
be a bracket (fixed on the cabinet) under the 
Ethernet switch to prop up it. 
● Configuration Cable Connection 
Please know that the desktop Ethernet switch device 
supports DC 5-16V(non-PoE) and 44-57V(PoE) power 
supply.
