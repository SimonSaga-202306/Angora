Hi, Levent,
Thanks for your offer.
After internal check, conclusions are as below.
1, STI-288GPM is not working, MSTP is not supported if you look at the datasheet.
This model is for price-sensitive, individual users.
In most cases, I do not think it is good for your business.
2, STI-288DGGPM is supporting all the function except 1.1.6
The switch shall support fault protection critical alarm relay outputs.
In the attached photo, there is a variant with serial port(RS485, DI/DO).
1.1.6 is about DI/DO function, our engineers said now RS485 is mature but DI/DO is not.
Sorry for this.
3, Maybe you could get some units for some sample units for testing(STI-288DGGPM).
This model is hot-selling and there will be more RFPs about it.
And one important thing is,
There might be some functions missing/you possibly need, you could let us know.
Then we will check if possible to add it(this is about another email Taylan just sent me).
