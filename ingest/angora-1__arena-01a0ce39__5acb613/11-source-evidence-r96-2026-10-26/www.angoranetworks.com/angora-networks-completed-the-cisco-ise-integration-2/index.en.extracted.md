Ankara, Turkey – June 21, 2021 – Angora Networks ® , a disruptive Turkish manufacturer of Communications Networking equipment, Turkey’s leading and innovative Network equipment manufacturers Angora Networks ® completed the Cisco ISE integration.

PacketFence https://www.packetfence.org/ is a fully supported, reliable, Free and Open Source network access control (NAC) solution. It packs an impressive feature set that includes a captive portal for registration and remediation, centralized wired and wireless management, 802.1X support, layer 2 isolation of problematic devices, integration with Snort IDS and Nessus vulnerability scanner. PacketFence can be used to effectively protect all networks, from small to very large heterogeneous networks. End-to-end PacketFence support in Turkey is provided by Alaattin Bilişim http://www.alaattin.com.tr/.

Wıth the support of our valued partner Alaattın Bilişim, Angora Networks network switches are fully compatible with PacketFence NAC within the scope of 802.1x integration. All 802.1x features required by our valued customers are supported. It is now possible to have the PacketFence and Angora Networks solution delivered on a turnkey basis.

Angora Networks works hard to support its customers with the highest quality products and services. Angora Networks offers its customers its agility and power in software development to the advantage of the market. Angora Networks effectively applies its field experience and expertise gained as a result of many years to the solution of the business problems of the market and its customers. This approach is a win-win scenario for all stakeholders, especially users.
