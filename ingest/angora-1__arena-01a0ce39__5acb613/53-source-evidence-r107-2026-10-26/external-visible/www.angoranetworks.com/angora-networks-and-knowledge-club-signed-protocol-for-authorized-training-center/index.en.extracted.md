Ankara, Turkey – February 12, 2021 **–** Turkey’s leading and innovative Communication Network equipment manufacturer Angora Networks ® signed a protocol with Knowledge Club, one of the leading network training centers, as an Authorized Training Center.

Knowledge Club (https://www.knowledgeclub.net/) With 15 years of field experience, it provides necessary solution services to its business partners and customers with full innovation in Europe, Middle East, Asia and Africa networks, systems, big data, programming, is an IT company that provides training, consultancy and application services in the fields of cloud solutions and software development. The Knowledge Club has its own office in 3 different countries, Turkey, Ukraine and Slovakia.

Through our close and strong partnership with the Knowledge Club, Angora Networks prides itself on providing both theoretical and practical training on its products and services. The curriculum is multilingual, including Turkish and English.
