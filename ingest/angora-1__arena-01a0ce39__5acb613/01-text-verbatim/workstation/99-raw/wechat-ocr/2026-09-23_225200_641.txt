The key structural observations from the data:
Types 3, 4, 5 are nearly identical in features — the only differences are that Type 4 has no stacking
requirement, and Type 5 adds the industrial temperature/fanless constraint.
Types 1 and 2 are L2+ only — no routing protocols, no PIM, no QoS-per-interface. The difference is just the
Add-on variant in Type 2.
Type 6 is the outlier — fiber-only ports, much higher performance (128 Mpps / 196 Gbps), adds BGP, drops
STP/RSTP/MSTP, and requires redundant PSU. It also does not explicitly require 802.1X or TACACS+.
