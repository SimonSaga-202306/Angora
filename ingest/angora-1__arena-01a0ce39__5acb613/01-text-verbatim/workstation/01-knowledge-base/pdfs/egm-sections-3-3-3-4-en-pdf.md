3.3  FIELD NETWORK SWITCH 
(Original Turkish heading: Saha Ağ Anahtarı) 
Clause Requirement 
3.3.1. The Field Network Switch shall be of industrial type. 
3.3.2. The Field Network Switch shall have a minimum of 8 (eight) 10/100/1000 Ethernet ports and a 
minimum of 2 (two) 1000 Base-X SFP ports. For the 1000 Base-X ports, compatible SFP modules 
supporting a distance of at least 1 (one) km shall be supplied pre-installed, of the same brand or 
approved by the manufacturer. 
3.3.3. The operating temperature range of the Field Network Switch shall be a minimum of -40 (minus 
forty) °C to +60 (plus sixty) °C. 
3.3.4. The Field Network Switch shall be capable of operating in a relative humidity environment of at 
least 10% (ten percent) to 95% (ninety-five percent). 
3.3.5. The Field Network Switch shall feature fanless cooling. There shall be no moving-part cooling 
system. 
3.3.6. The Field Network Switch shall have a minimum IP20 rating. 
3.3.7. The Field Network Switch shall support fault-protection alarm relay outputs. 
3.3.8. A compatible fiber patch cable of at least 1 (one) metre in length shall be supplied for each SFP 
used on the Field Network Switch. 
3.3.9. All ports on the Field Network Switch shall operate in non-blocking mode. 
3.3.10. The Field Network Switch shall support a minimum of 8,000 (eight thousand) MAC addresses. 
3.3.11. The Field Network Switch shall have 4 (four) priority queues. 
3.3.12. The Field Network Switch shall support RMON and SNMP v1, v2, and v3. The Field Network 
Switch shall be manageable via Telnet, console port, and web interface. 
3.3.13. The Field Network Switch shall support ICMP and IGMP snooping at Layer 2. 
3.3.14. The Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1Q VLAN. It shall also support Multi Spanning-
Tree Protocol (MSTP) (IEEE 802.1s), x-ring or equivalent protocol, and VLAN tagging/VLAN 
trunking. 
3.3.15. The Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1p prioritisation and the ToS standard. 
3.3.16. Port security based on MAC address shall be achievable on the Field Network Switch. 
3.3.17. The Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1x port-based network access control, permitting 
only authorised users to communicate via ports.

3.3.18. The Field Network Switch shall support port mirroring. 
3.3.19. Every 10/100/1000 Ethernet port on the Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.3af (PoE) 
and/or IEEE 802.3at (PoE+). 
3.3.20. The Field Network Switch shall support Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP, IEEE 802.3ad),

3.3.21. The Field Network Switch shall support IPv6, VLAN interface IP addressing, and traffic

3.3.22. Field Network Switches shall all be of the same brand and model. 
3.4  INDOOR NETWORK SWITCH 
(Original Turkish heading: İç Ortam Ağ Anahtarı) 
Clause Requirement 
3.4.1. The Indoor Network Switch shall have a minimum of 24 (twenty-four) 10/100/1000 Gigabit Ethernet 
ports and a minimum of 4 (four) 1000 Base-X SFP ports. For the 1000 Base-X ports, 4 (four) 
compatible SFP modules supporting a distance of at least 10 (ten) km, of the same brand or 
approved by the manufacturer, shall be supplied pre-installed. 
3.4.2. All 10/100/1000 Base-TX ports of the Indoor Network Switch shall operate with auto-negotiate, full 
duplex, half duplex, and active settings. 
3.4.3. All 10/100/1000 Base-TX ports on the Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.3af and/or 
802.3at (PoE or PoE+). 
3.4.4. The operating temperature range of the Indoor Network Switch shall be a minimum of 0 (zero) °C 
to +45 (plus forty-five) °C. 
3.4.5. The Indoor Network Switch shall be capable of operating in a relative humidity environment of at 
least 10% (ten percent) to 85% (eighty-five percent). 
3.4.6. The switching (backplane) capacity of the Indoor Network Switch shall be a minimum of 56 (fifty-
six) Gbps. 
3.4.7. The packet forwarding performance of the Indoor Network Switch shall be a minimum of 41.7 
(forty-one point seven) Mpps. 
3.4.8. The MAC address table of the Indoor Network Switch shall support a minimum of 16,000 (sixteen 
thousand) MAC addresses. 
3.4.9. The Indoor Network Switch shall support the IEEE 802.1d Spanning Tree Protocol. 
3.4.10. The Indoor Network Switch shall support the BPDU guard feature. 
3.4.11. The Indoor Network Switch shall support the IEEE 802.3x feature for traffic flow control. 
3.4.12. The Indoor Network Switch shall support the IEEE 802.3ad Link Aggregation standard. 
3.4.13. A minimum of 4 (four) trunk groups (Trunk or LAG) shall be configurable on the Indoor Network 
Switch, each containing a minimum of 8 (eight) ports. 
3.4.14. The Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1Q VLAN standards. The Indoor Network 
Switch shall support a minimum VLAN ID of 4K and a minimum of 1,000 (one thousand) VLANs. 
3.4.15. The Indoor Network Switch shall support jumbo frames of at least 9,000 (nine thousand) bytes. 
3.4.16. The Indoor Network Switch shall support the IEEE 802.1p standard. 
3.4.17. The Indoor Network Switch shall be capable of performing queuing operations based on Layer 2, 
Layer 3, and Layer 4 protocol types. 
3.4.18. The Indoor Network Switch shall support DSCP marking and shall perform IP-based QoS services 
according to DSCP.

Clause Requirement 
3.4.19. The Indoor Network Switch shall support Port Rate Limiting. 
3.4.20. Each port on the Indoor Network Switch shall support a minimum of 4 (four) Priority Queues and 
Weighted Round Robin or equivalent protocol support. 
3.4.21. The Indoor Network Switch shall support IGMP Snooping v1 and v2. 
3.4.22. A minimum of 255 (two hundred and fifty-five) Multicast groups shall be configurable on the Indoor 
Network Switch. 
3.4.23. The Indoor Network Switch shall be capable of creating Access Control Lists (ACLs) based on 
Layer 2 MAC address, Layer 3 IP address, and Layer 4 protocol information. 
3.4.24. A minimum of 200 (two hundred) access control rules shall be configurable on the Indoor Network 
Switch. 
3.4.25. The Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1x Port Based Authentication and MAC Based 
Authentication to authorise users connecting to the network for the purpose of ensuring network 
security. 
3.4.26. The Indoor Network Switch shall support dynamic VLAN assignment using 802.1x. The Indoor 
Network Switch shall also support Guest VLAN. 
3.4.27. The Indoor Network Switch shall support port mirroring for the purpose of analysing traffic passing 
through it. 
3.4.28. The Indoor Network Switch shall have IPv4 Static Routing capability. 
3.4.29. The Indoor Network Switch shall support SSH. 
3.4.30. The Indoor Network Switch shall support SNMP v1, v2, and v3. It shall also be manageable via 
console port and web browser. 
3.4.31. The Indoor Network Switch shall support RMON 4 groups (Stats, History, Alarms, and Events). 
3.4.32. The Indoor Network Switch shall support DHCP Relay, DHCP Snooping, and DHCP Option 82. 
3.4.33. The Indoor Network Switch shall support port security. 
3.4.34. The Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1ab Link Layer Discovery Protocol (LLDP). 
3.4.35. The Indoor Network Switch shall support SYSLOG. 
3.4.36. The Indoor Network Switch shall support IPv6. 
3.4.37. The Indoor Network Switch shall support Radius and TACACS+. 
3.4.38. The Indoor Network Switch shall be offered with the most current and advanced Firmware. 
3.4.39. The Indoor Network Switch shall have a minimum of 128 (one hundred and twenty-eight) MB of 
RAM and 16 (sixteen) MB of flash memory. 
3.4.40. Multiple configuration files shall be storable on the Indoor Network Switch, and the Indoor Network 
Switch firmware shall be updatable via TFTP. 
3.4.41. All licences required for all features and protocols specified for the Indoor Network Switch to 
operate actively shall be included in the offer. 
3.4.42. All equipment required for the operation of the Indoor Network Switch (power cable, etc.), cabinet 
mounting accessories, and CAT6 cables shall be delivered together with the switch. 
3.4.43. The Indoor Network Switch shall support SNTP (Simple Network Time Protocol) or NTP (Network 
Time Protocol) for the purpose of synchronising with all other switches on the network. 
3.4.44. Indoor Network Switches shall all be of the same brand and model.
