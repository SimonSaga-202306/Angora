1.1. C TYPE ETHERNET SWITCH 
Within the scope of this specification, it is the communication device where 
camera images are collected in the field cabinets located on the poles. This 
switch can be of a different brand than other switches. 
1.1.1. The device to be proposed will be an Industrial type switch where cameras, 
outdoor wireless access devices and network devices will be collected in field 
cabinets.  
1.1.2. The operating temperature conditions of the switches shall be at least between 
-40o C and +60 C. o 
1.1.3. Will be able to work in at least 10-95% relative humidity environments.  
1.1.4. It will have fanless cooling feature. There will be no cooling system with 
moving parts on it.  
1.1.5. It shall support at least IP20 ambient protection standard.  
1.1.6. The switch shall support fault protection critical alarm relay outputs.  
1.1.7. It will support redundant ring ethernet feature by working in Ring and Mesh 
ethernet networks. In case of fiber cable or switch failure at any point on the 
fiber route, the rest of the ethernet backbone will be able to continue to operate 
without interruption. 
1.1.8. The switch will have at least 8 (eight) Gigabit Ethernet ports and at least 2 
(two) fiber Gigabit Ethernet ports.  Fiber ports can be used by selecting GBIC 
modules suitable for the connection distance, GBIC/SFP modules operating up 
to 70 (seventy) Km can be installed and 1000 BASE LX, SX, ZX and 
100BASE-X fiber interfaces can be installed. The switch shall be equipped 
with 1 (one) 10 (ten) km supported 1000 Base-LX SFP of the same brand as 
itself. In case the distance to be transferred exceeds 10 (ten) km; the appropriate 
SFP shall be provided by the Contractor. 
1.1.9. A 1 meter fiber patch cable compatible with the number of SFPs used or offered 
on the switch to be offered will be provided.  
1.1.10. All ports on the switch will operate as non-blocking. 
1.1.11. The device shall support at least 8,000 (eight thousand) MAC addresses. 
1.1.12. The device will have 4 (four) priority queues. 
1.1.13. The device will have RMON and SNMP v1, v2 and v3 support. The device 
will be managed via telnet, console port and web interface.  
1.1.14. The device will support ICMP and IGMP snooping at L2 level.  
1.1.15. The device shall have IEEE 802.1Q VLAN support. MSTP (IEEE 802.1s), x-
ring or similar protocol and VLAN tagging/VLAN trunking support 
1.1.16. It shall support IEEE 802.1p prioritization and ToS standard.  
1.1.17. Port security will be provided on MAC address basis. 
1.1.18. The device (and the power adapter supplied with the device, if any) shall be 
rail mountable. 
1.1.19. With IEEE 802.1x port-based network access control, only authorized users 
will be able to support communication over the port.  
1.1.20. Port mirroring will be supported. 
1.1.21. Each port on the device will have IEEE 802.3af (PoE) and IEEE 802.3at 
(PoE+) features. 
1.1.22. With Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP IEEE 802.3ad) support, high 
bandwidth can be provided by using multiple Ethernet ports virtually as a 
single trunk port. 
1.1.23. The device will support IPv6; vlan interface IP addressing and traffic.
