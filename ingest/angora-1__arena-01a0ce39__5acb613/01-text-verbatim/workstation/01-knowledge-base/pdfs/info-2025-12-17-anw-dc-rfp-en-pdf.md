ANNEX-1 
TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT 
NETWORK SWITCH SYSTEMS 
Network Switch Systems Requirements 
Type-1 and Type-2 Network Switches will be supplied for use in the User’s Data Centre. The 
specifications of Type-1 and Type-2 Network Switches are detailed in Annex-5 
(CUSTOMER Hardware and Software Requirements Document) on the Network Switches 
page. 
Type-1 and Type-2 Network Switches shall be delivered with the necessary modules and 
cables to operate in a redundant (stacked) configuration where two (2) switches form a single 
group. 
Type-1 48 x 1/10/25G Fiber + 6 x 40/100G Fiber 
Network Switch Minimum Performance Requirements 
• Switching capacity: at least 3.6 Tbps 
• Packet forwarding rate: at least 1 Bpps 
• Minimum 128 GB SSD storage and 16 GB RAM 
• Port buffer: at least 32 MB 
• Shall be installed in MLAG (Multi-Chassis Link Aggregation) configuration for 
redundancy at each location 
• MLAG peer-link shall use 100 Gbps connections (via SFP or DAC cables) 
• MTBF value: at least 300,000 hours 
• MAC address table size: at least 294,000 entries 
• Shall support simultaneous port mirroring of at least 4 different traffic flows 
• Each switch shall be equipped with redundant (dual) power supplies 
Type-2  48 x 1G Copper RJ45 + 4x 10G Fiber 
Network Switch Minimum Performance Requirements 
• Shall operate in stack configuration with stack bandwidth of at least 40 Gbps 
• Up to 8 devices shall be connectable in a single stack and manageable via a single IP 
address 
• Each switch shall connect redundantly to the headquarters network and shall include 
the required optics (10G-SR) for this uplink 
• Per-switch switching capacity: at least 176 Gbps 
• Per-switch packet forwarding rate: at least 130 Mpps 
• Port buffer: at least 4 MB per switch 
• Minimum 1 GB DRAM and 1 GB Flash memory per switch 
• MACsec support required 
• Shall support the following protocols and features: Static Routing, OSPF, Policy-
Based Routing (PBR), Private VLAN, VRRP, QoS, 802.1X

Delivery and Installation Requirements 
• Switches shall be updated to the latest stable firmware version before delivery 
• Network settings, VLANs, LACP, SSH, SNMP, and NTP configurations shall be 
performed in accordance with the Analysis Activity Report 
• All necessary cabinet mounting hardware, power cables, and network cables (of any 
type) required during installation shall be supplied by the Contractor in compliance 
with the User Data Centre standards 
• Uplink modules required for interoperability with existing network switches at the 
headquarters location (as specified for Type-1 and Type-2 switches in Annex-5, 
Network Switches page) shall be provided by the Contractor 
The switches shall be delivered fully ready for operation.
