Section 3.3 — Network Switching Equipment 
English Translation of Technical Specification Clauses 
3.3.1  Indoor (Premises) Network Switch 
3.3.1.1 The Indoor Network Switch shall have a minimum of 24 (twenty-four) 
10/100/1000 Gigabit Ethernet ports and a minimum of 4 (four) 1000Base-X SFP 
ports. The 1000Base-X SFP ports shall be offered with 2 (two) SFP transceivers 
supporting 10 (ten) km reach of the same brand as the switch or manufacturer-
approved equivalents. 
3.3.1.2 All 10/100/1000Base-TX ports of the Indoor Network Switch shall operate in 
auto-negotiate, full-duplex, and active mode. 
3.3.1.3 All copper 10/100/1000Base-TX ports of the Indoor Network Switch shall 
support IEEE 802.3af (PoE) and/or 802.3at (PoE+). 
3.3.1.4 The operating temperature range of the Indoor Network Switch shall be a 
minimum of 0 (zero) °C to +45 (forty-five) °C. 
3.3.1.5 The Indoor Network Switch shall be capable of operating in a relative humidity 
environment of at least 10% (ten percent) to 85% (eighty-five percent). 
3.3.1.6 The switching (backplane) capacity of the Indoor Network Switch shall be a 
minimum of 56 (fifty-six) Gbps. Packet forwarding performance shall be a 
minimum of 41.7 (forty-one point seven) Mpps. 
3.3.1.7 The MAC address table of the Indoor Network Switch shall support a minimum 
of 16,000 (sixteen thousand) MAC addresses. 
3.3.1.8 The Indoor Network Switch shall support the IEEE 802.1d Spanning Tree 
Protocol. 
3.3.1.9 The Indoor Network Switch shall support BPDU Guard functionality. 
3.3.1.10 The Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.3x flow control to regulate 
traffic flow. 
3.3.1.11 The Indoor Network Switch shall support the IEEE 802.3ad Link Aggregation 
standard. A minimum of 4 (four) Link Aggregation Groups (Trunk / LAG) shall be 
configurable on the Indoor Network Switch, with a minimum of 8 (eight) ports per 
group. 
3.3.1.12 The Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1Q VLAN standards. The 
Indoor Network Switch shall support a minimum of 4K VLAN IDs and a minimum 
of 1,000 (one thousand) VLANs. 
3.3.1.13 The Indoor Network Switch shall support a minimum jumbo frame size of 9,000 
(nine thousand) bytes. 
3.3.1.14 The Indoor Network Switch shall support the IEEE 802.1p standard. 
3.3.1.15 The Indoor Network Switch shall be capable of performing queuing operations 
at L2, L3, and L4 protocol levels. 
3.3.1.16 The Indoor Network Switch shall support DSCP marking and shall provide IP-
based QoS service based on DSCP values.

3.3.1.17 The Indoor Network Switch shall support port rate limiting (Port Rate Limiting). 
Each port on the Indoor Network Switch shall support a minimum of 4 (four) 
priority queues and shall support Weighted Round Robin or an equivalent 
protocol. 
3.3.1.18 The Indoor Network Switch shall support IGMP Snooping v1 and v2. 
3.3.1.19 A minimum of 255 (two hundred and fifty-five) Multicast groups shall be 
configurable on the Indoor Network Switch. 
3.3.1.20 The Indoor Network Switch shall be capable of creating Access Control Lists 
(ACLs) based on L2 MAC addresses, L3 IP addresses, and L4 protocol 
information. A minimum of 200 (two hundred) access control rules shall be 
configurable on the Indoor Network Switch. 
3.3.1.21 The Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1x Port-Based 
Authentication and MAC-Based Authentication for the authorisation of users 
connecting to the network to ensure network security. 
3.3.1.22 The Indoor Network Switch shall support dynamic VLAN assignment using 
802.1x. Additionally, the Indoor Network Switch shall support Guest VLAN. 
3.3.1.23 The Indoor Network Switch shall support port mirroring for the purpose of 
analysing traffic passing through it. 
3.3.1.24 The Indoor Network Switch shall support IPv4 Static Routing. 
3.3.1.25 The Indoor Network Switch shall support SSH. 
3.3.1.26 The Indoor Network Switch shall support SNMP v1, v2, and v3. It shall 
additionally be manageable via console port and web browser. 
3.3.1.27 The Indoor Network Switch shall support RMON 4 Groups. 
3.3.1.28 The Indoor Network Switch shall support DHCP Relay, DHCP Snooping, and 
DHCP Option 82. 
3.3.1.29 The Indoor Network Switch shall support port security. 
3.3.1.30 The Indoor Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1ab Link Layer Discovery 
Protocol (LLDP). 
3.3.1.31 The Indoor Network Switch shall support SYSLOG. 
3.3.1.32 The Indoor Network Switch shall support IPv6. 
3.3.1.33 The Indoor Network Switch shall support Radius and TACACS+. 
3.3.1.34 The Indoor Network Switch shall be offered with the latest and most advanced 
firmware version available. 
3.3.1.35 The Indoor Network Switch shall have a minimum of 128 (one hundred and 
twenty-eight) MB RAM and 16 (sixteen) MB flash memory. 
3.3.1.36 Multiple configuration files shall be storable on the Indoor Network Switch, and 
the switch firmware shall be upgradeable via TFTP. 
3.3.1.37 All licences required for all features and protocols specified herein to operate 
actively on the Indoor Network Switch shall be included in the offer. 
3.3.1.38 All equipment required for the operation of the Indoor Network Switch (power 
cable, etc.), cabinet mounting materials, and CAT6 cables shall be supplied 
together. 
3.3.1.39 The Indoor Network Switch shall support SNTP (Simple Network Time Protocol) 
or NTP (Network Time Protocol) for synchronisation with all other switches on 
the network.

3.3.2  Field Network Switch 
3.3.2.1 The Field Network Switch shall be of industrial type. 
3.3.2.2 The Field Network Switch shall have a minimum of 8 (eight) 10/100/1000 
Ethernet ports and a minimum of 2 (two) 1000Base-X SFP ports. The 
1000Base-X SFP ports shall be offered with 1 (one) SFP transceiver supporting 
10 (ten) km reach of the same brand as the switch or manufacturer-approved 
equivalents. 
3.3.2.3 The operating temperature range of the Field Network Switch shall be a 
minimum of -40 (minus forty) °C to +60 (plus sixty) °C. 
3.3.2.4 The Field Network Switch shall be capable of operating in a relative humidity 
environment of at least 10% (ten percent) to 95% (ninety-five percent). 
3.3.2.5 The Field Network Switch shall have fanless cooling. It shall not have a cooling 
system with moving parts. 
3.3.2.6 The Field Network Switch shall comply with a minimum of the IP20 standard. 
3.3.2.7 The Field Network Switch shall support critical fault protection alarm relay 
outputs. 
3.3.2.8 A minimum of 1 (one) metre fibre optic patch cable compatible with the number 
of SFP ports used on the Field Network Switch shall be delivered. 
3.3.2.9 All ports on the Field Network Switch shall operate on a non-blocking basis. 
3.3.2.10 The Field Network Switch shall support a minimum of 8,000 (eight thousand) 
MAC addresses. 
3.3.2.11 The Field Network Switch shall have 4 (four) priority queues. 
3.3.2.12 The Field Network Switch shall support RMON and SNMP v1, v2, and v3. The 
Field Network Switch shall be manageable via Telnet, console port, and web 
interface. 
3.3.2.13 The Field Network Switch shall support ICMP and IGMP Snooping at L2 level. 
3.3.2.14 The Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1Q VLAN. It shall support 
Multi Spanning-Tree Protocol (MSTP) (IEEE 802.1s), x-ring or an equivalent 
protocol, and VLAN tagging/VLAN trunking. 
3.3.2.15 The Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1p prioritisation and ToS 
standard. 
3.3.2.16 Port security shall be enforceable on the Field Network Switch on a per-MAC-
address basis. 
3.3.2.17 The Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 802.1x port-based network access 
control to allow only authorised users to communicate over the port. 
3.3.2.18 The Field Network Switch shall support port mirroring. 
3.3.2.19 Each 10/100/1000 Ethernet port on the Field Network Switch shall support IEEE 
802.3af (PoE) and/or IEEE 802.3at (PoE+). 
3.3.2.20 The Field Network Switch shall support Link Aggregation Control Protocol 
(LACP — IEEE 802.3ad) to aggregate multiple Ethernet ports virtually into a 
single trunk port for increased bandwidth. 
3.3.2.21 The Field Network Switch shall support IPv6 VLAN interface IP addressing and 
traffic. 
3.3.2.22 The Field Network Switch and its power adapter shall be DIN-rail mountable. 
The Field Network Switch shall be delivered with the power adapter already 
connected and complete with all accessories in a single enclosure.
