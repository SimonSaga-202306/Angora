TRANSLATION OF SECTION 3.3 — EDGE ETHERNET SWITCH 
Scope of this document 
This document presents a verbatim English translation of Section 3.3 (Second Part — Edge Ethernet Switch) of 
the KGYS Modernisation Project Material Procurement Technical Specification, approved on 12/02/2026 by 
Mehmet ÇATAL, Head of the Information Technologies and Communications Department, First-Class Police 
Commissioner. 
 
3.3  SECOND PART — EDGE ETHERNET SWITCH 
3.3.1  GENERAL REQUIREMENTS 
3.3.1.1  The Contractor shall deliver, together with each edge ethernet switch, an industrial-type 
adapter and/or transformer that is compatible with the switch, supports the IEEE 802.3at (PoE+) 
power values conforming to the mains voltage standards used in Turkey, and can be mounted on 
a DIN rail — to be delivered to the Inspection and Acceptance Commission at the acceptance 
stage. 
3.3.1.2  The Contractor shall deliver, at the acceptance stage, one (1) 1000 Base-LX SFP module 
per edge ethernet switch (supporting 10 km link distance) to the Inspection and Acceptance 
Commission. The aforementioned SFP modules may be delivered either in the device box or in 
separate boxes. 
3.3.1.3  The Contractor shall deliver, at the acceptance stage, one (1) SC-LC fibre patch cable of 
1 (one) metre length per edge ethernet switch — compatible with the edge ethernet switch — to 
the Inspection and Acceptance Commission. 
3.3.1.4  The equipment specified in clauses 3.3.1.1, 3.3.1.2, and 3.3.1.3 shall be delivered in a 
single box together with the edge ethernet switch. 
3.3.1.5  All edge ethernet switches offered shall support multicast transmission. 
3.3.2  EDGE ETHERNET SWITCH 
3.3.2.1  The edge ethernet switch shall be of industrial type. 
3.3.2.2  The edge ethernet switch shall be fanless (passive cooling). It shall not have any moving 
parts for cooling. 
3.3.2.3  The edge ethernet switch shall be DIN-rail mountable. 
3.3.2.4  The edge ethernet switch shall support critical alarm relay outputs for fault protection.

3.3.2.5  The edge ethernet switch shall support the redundant ring Ethernet feature, operating in 
ring and mesh Ethernet networks. 
3.3.2.6  The edge ethernet switch shall have at least 8 (eight) Gigabit Ethernet ports and at least 
2 (two) fibre Gigabit Ethernet ports. Fibre ports shall accommodate GBIC modules appropriate for 
the required connection distance; GBIC/SFP modules operating up to 70 (seventy) km and 1000 
BASE LX, SX, ZX and 100 BASE-X fibre interfaces shall be installable. 
3.3.2.7  All ports on the edge ethernet switch shall operate in non-blocking mode. 
3.3.2.8  The edge ethernet switch shall support port security on a MAC address basis. 
3.3.2.9  The edge ethernet switch shall support IEEE 802.1x port-based network access control, 
enabling only authorised users to communicate through the port. 
3.3.2.10  The edge ethernet switch shall support port mirroring. 
3.3.2.11  Each port on the edge ethernet switch shall feature IEEE 802.3af (PoE) and IEEE 
802.3at (PoE+) functionality. 
3.3.2.12  The edge ethernet switch shall support at least 8,000 (eight thousand) MAC addresses. 
3.3.2.13  The edge ethernet switch shall have at least 4 (four) priority queues. 
3.3.2.14  The edge ethernet switch shall support RMON and SNMP v1, v2, and v3, and shall be 
manageable via telnet, console port, and web interface. 
3.3.2.15  The edge ethernet switch shall support ICMP and IGMP snooping at Layer 2. 
3.3.2.16  The edge ethernet switch shall support IEEE 802.1Q VLAN. It shall support MSTP 
(IEEE 802.1s), x-ring or an equivalent protocol, and VLAN tagging/VLAN trunking. 
3.3.2.17  The edge ethernet switch shall support IEEE 802.1p priority and the ToS standard. 
3.3.2.18  The edge ethernet switch shall support Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP IEEE 
802.3ad), enabling multiple Ethernet ports to be used virtually as a single trunk port for higher 
bandwidth. 
3.3.2.19  The edge ethernet switch shall support IPv6, VLAN interface IP addressing, and IPv6 
traffic. 
3.3.2.20  The edge ethernet switch shall be delivered with its licences loaded and shall not require 
any additional licence for performing its designated functions. 
3.3.2.21  The edge ethernet switch shall operate with all its functions within the temperature range 
of -40 (minus forty) °C to +60 (plus sixty) °C. 
3.3.2.22  The edge ethernet switch shall be capable of operating at up to 95% (ninety-five per 
cent) relative humidity. 
3.3.2.23  The edge ethernet switch shall comply with at least IP20 standard. 
3.3.2.24  All edge ethernet switches offered shall hold a "C-Type Edge Switch and Traffic 
Shaping Test Report" with result "SUCCESSFUL", obtained from the General Directorate of 
Security, Department of Information Technologies and Communications. Reports 
previously issued under the name of the Communications Department shall also be 
accepted as valid. The said report shall be presented to the Inspection and Acceptance 
Commission at the acceptance stage.
