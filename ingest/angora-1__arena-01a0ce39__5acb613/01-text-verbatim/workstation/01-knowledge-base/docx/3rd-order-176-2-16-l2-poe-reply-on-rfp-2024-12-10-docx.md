There are some interesting features that may help stand out.
1, hardware material
1.1
Expert Design
The upper panel, aluminum
The other sides, iron
This ensures better dissipation especially for PoE models
Though this model STI-280DGGM is Non-PoE.
1.2
Push-in Terminal Block
Now on the market many suppliers are doing traditional screw-type terminal block as below
This is more difficult in installation than push-in type
On top of this, the heat sink part&EMI protection is better than peer suppliers.
You have the 2 units and can easily understand those points.
It is not necessarily helpful in this project, but maybe.
And this is exactly we are better than competitors.
2, hardware features
2.1
Serial function
Our engineer suggested this to be mandatory in this tender.
As far as we know, few peer suppliers have a full range of industrial switches with serial function.
Some suppliers just have 1 or 2 models while we have many.At the same time, the price is not expensive after adding it.
Meanwhile, there is USB&Alarm function available,
We think this is critical to stand out from this tender.
2.2
Chip info
It is Marvell Based IC and we develop our own software(codes)
For most peer suppliers, they do Realtek, Vitesse or Broadcom
Moreover, the software is foreign, that is to say, when there is a substantial software change, they are very passive and subject to the software supplier.
For us, we can easily change most of the requested features(of course cost and R&D time is a big concern), but technically its very possible.
Our engineer suggested maybe add a term “have the capability to do software change for future purpose if any”, some kind of description like this to stand out
3, software features
3.1

For Huawei, H3C those big guys, they are not in this competition, right?
So this gives us an edge.

Additional features
